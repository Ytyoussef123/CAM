Run & teach

—

Put a part and press Trigger: the image is taken from the live view and inspected. Then tell the sensor, area by area, if it is OK or NG and press Learn: the thresholds adjust to your examples.

Learned images
How it decides: inside each control area the image is compared pixel by pixel with the good examples of that area. Each area has a threshold: above it the area is NG, and one NG area makes the part NG. When you teach OK and NG examples, the threshold is placed between the highest OK score and the lowest NG score. Parts must sit in the same place each time and the light must stay the same.
