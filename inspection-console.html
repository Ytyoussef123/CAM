Teach

—

After a trigger, tell the sensor, area by area, if the part is OK or NG and press Learn: OK examples raise the scores of good parts, NG examples lower the scores of bad ones.

Learned images
How it decides: each area gets a score from 0 to 100: 100 means it looks like the good examples. Below its limit (50 by default) the area is NG, and one NG area makes the part NG. When you teach OK and NG examples, good parts score higher and bad parts lower. Parts must sit in the same place each time (or use a ⌖ position reference) and the light must stay the same.
